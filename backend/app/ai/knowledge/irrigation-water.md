# Irrigation and water management

## Critical growth stages
Water stress hurts yield most at specific stages: crown root initiation and flowering in wheat; tillering, panicle initiation and flowering in rice; flowering and pod filling in pulses; and silking in maize. When water is scarce, prioritise irrigation at these stages.

## Drip and sprinkler irrigation
Drip irrigation delivers water to the root zone and can save 30 to 50% of water compared with flood irrigation, while improving yields of fruits, vegetables, cotton and sugarcane. Fertilizers can be applied through the drip system (fertigation). Subsidies for micro-irrigation are available under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana.

## Rainfall and drainage
Rainfall needs differ widely: rice and jute need high rainfall, while chickpea, moth bean and mung bean are suited to low rainfall. Fields growing pulses, maize and vegetables must be well drained, because waterlogging quickly damages their roots. Mulching reduces evaporation in dry periods.
