# Crop growing guides

## Rice
Rice is a kharif cereal that needs high humidity, temperatures of about 20 to 35 °C and plenty of water, with rainfall above roughly 150 to 200 mm in the growing months or assured irrigation. It grows in slightly acidic to neutral soils. Transplanted rice is commonly given nitrogen in three splits; alternate wetting and drying irrigation saves water without reducing yield.

## Maize
Maize is grown in kharif and, with irrigation, in rabi and spring. It needs well-drained fertile soil, moderate rainfall and good nitrogen supply. Waterlogging, even for a short period, damages the crop.

## Chickpea
Chickpea is a rabi pulse suited to cool, dry weather and grows well on residual soil moisture. It needs little nitrogen because of biological nitrogen fixation, but responds to phosphorus. Seed treatment with Rhizobium culture improves nodulation. Excess irrigation and humid weather encourage wilt and blight.

## Cotton
Cotton is a kharif fibre crop that needs a long frost-free period, warm temperatures and moderate rainfall, and grows well on black cotton soils. It has a high nitrogen requirement given in splits. Monitor regularly for pink bollworm and sucking pests.

## Fruit crops
Banana needs warm, humid conditions with high nitrogen and potassium. Grapes and apple need high phosphorus and potassium; apple needs a cold winter for chilling. Pomegranate tolerates drought and suits semi-arid regions. Mango is grown widely and needs a dry period before flowering.
